## Rules

- **Must** — The approved 12-screen reference board is the visual authority; generated alternatives may not silently replace its composition, warmth or light/dark relationship.
- **Must** — Today shows the baby before planning information; BabyHero is the product signature, not an optional promotional card.
- **Must** — Images are first-class design-system assets with family, crop, focal point, safe area, loading, error, reduced-motion and medical-review metadata.
- **Must** — Weeks 5 through 40 use one coherent baby universe: consistent lighting, lens, materials, quality and treatment.
- **Must** — Every weekly embryo or fetus visual requires medical review before production release and must never be presented as diagnostic imagery.
- **Must** — Postpartum prefers the family's own baby photo; the fallback is neutral material/light imagery, never a generated replacement child.
- **Must** — Preparation object photography uses the same warm neutral studio background, soft shadow direction, crop and product scale.
- **Must** — Light mode uses warm ivory, dusty rose, sand and very low-opacity botanical or organic edge artwork; it must not become a pastel baby app.
- **Must** — Dark mode uses deep warm navy-charcoal, cinematic domestic photography and restrained colored light; no outer-space, neon or generic sci-fi treatment.
- **Must** — Primary actions and navigation remain neutral navy/ink across gender states.
- **Must** — Boy or girl selection changes contextual accents, progress and selected states only; canvas and shared surfaces remain neutral.
- **Must** — Gender recording always includes later and prefer-not-to-record paths and never uses stereotypes.
- **Must** — Primary navigation contains exactly Today, Journey, Preparation and More; finance lives behind the father's authorized access.
- **Must** — Mobile screens use a four-column grid, 20px page gutters, 12px grid gaps and 24px section rhythm.
- **Must** — Use geometry, imagery and spacing before adding color; one screen may have only one dominant visual and one secondary accent.
- **Should** — Cards use tone and hairline borders by default; raised shadows are reserved for floating navigation, sheets and explicit elevation.
- **Must** — Controls use 14px, cards 20px, heroes 28px and pills only for chips, avatars and truly circular actions.
- **Must** — The app is an assistant, not a dashboard: avoid dense charts, repeated KPIs and same-size card grids.
- **Must** — Progress is always paired with a plain-language amount, state or next action; decorative progress alone is not allowed.
- **Must** — Father-private prices, totals, savings and monthly requirements are excluded at authorization and serialization layers, not hidden with CSS.
- **Must** — Financial calculations use funding date; spending date remains a separate descriptive field.
- **Must** — The system never changes a funding goal or allocation without explicit user confirmation and explains every recalculation.
- **Must** — Birth transitions the experience from pregnancy week to baby age; all pregnancy milestones remain available in journey history.
- **Must** — A journey view highlights exactly one current point and visually quiets past and future milestones.
- **Must** — BabyHero expands from its own geometry and media playback continues without a cut or restart.
- **Must** — Motion is soft, fluid, confident and continuous; bounce, confetti and cute elastic effects are forbidden.
- **Must** — Every video and shared-element transition has a meaningful reduced-motion path using the poster and a 120ms opacity transition.
- **Must** — Load the current weekly media only, prefetch adjacent media on suitable connections and fall back to the poster without removing text.
- **Should** — A screen uses at most two living visual effects; BabyHero counts as one.
- **Must** — Final icons share 20/24px optical sizes, approximately 1.6px stroke, softened-square geometry and selective active fill; unmodified Lucide or mixed libraries may not ship.
- **Must** — Emoji are not product icons or visual assets.
- **Must** — Layout, icon direction, transitions and mixed-number runs are designed for RTL rather than blindly mirrored.
- **Must** — Dates, amounts, percentages and dimensions use isolated bidirectional runs and tabular numerals where alignment matters.
- **Must** — Every interactive target is at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
- **Must** — Essential normal text meets WCAG AA contrast; faint color is never used for essential small text.
- **Must** — Interactive components define default, pressed, focus-visible and disabled states plus loading/error when they fetch media or data.
- **Must** — The app provides editable general information and planning support; it does not diagnose or replace the doctor.
- **Must** — Insurance coverage records show last-checked date and create a verification task instead of claiming guaranteed coverage.
- **Must** — Feeding flows support direct breastfeeding and pumped milk without assuming formula; formula appears only when the family chooses or medically needs it.
- **Must** — Follow-up city and delivery city are independent fields across hospital, doctor, travel and task flows.
- **Must** — Pregnancy is sensitive data; collect only information that materially helps tracking, planning or care coordination.
- **Must** — Production components reference semantic color roles rather than raw hex values; raw values exist only in token definitions and resolved previews.
- **Must** — Headings use medium or semibold weights; heavy black Arabic weights and rounded childish fonts are forbidden.
- **Must** — First view stays short; details appear through expansion, sheets or contextual pages rather than being stacked into the home screen.
- **Must** — Generated screen images are composition and art-direction references, not production source files; implementation must resolve to system tokens and accessible components.
- **Must** — Arabic copy rendered inside generated references must be reviewed and re-typeset in the product before release; screenshots never become the copy source of truth.
- **Must** — Each visual reference uses a stable route-or-state name so a regenerated screen replaces its predecessor instead of creating a duplicate.
- **Must** — Generated people are visual placeholders only and must never be represented as the user's family, doctor or child; real supplied media replaces them where identity matters.
